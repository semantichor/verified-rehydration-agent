# R3.5 Anchor attestation — AGT-VRA-001 — paradigm: prolog

Answers to the 4 buyer-contract questions, written after the R3.1 integral read and before R4/R5.

## Q1. What does the buyer-contract promise as delivered?

The pack delivers an execution-neutral, self-contained normative specification of the agent-logic surface for controlled rehydration of persistent context, sufficient to build the agent without further agent-logic design decisions (`Verified_Rehydration_Agent_README.md:12`). That surface comprises the controlled verdict vocabulary `ALLOW`, `DENY`, `QUARANTINE`, `ESCALATE`, `READMISSION_PENDING` (§6), the normative ordering and verification sequence (§7), the validator catalogue with severities and failure products (§10), the decision logic (§11), the `S0`–`S8` workflow and its transition model (§12), gate logic (§13), failure semantics (§8), and the evidence contract (§14). The README enumerates 7 structural properties grounded in spec §2 Core invariants — no restoration before the full verification sequence completes; fail-safe handling that suppresses persistent-context injection on any framing/checksum/parse/chain/signature/freshness/policy/IRR failure; restored state only from the verified and eligible head; tool-state inactive unless a separate activation gate and approval both succeed; startup read-only until the restoration decision; idempotent recovery; and a deterministic replay baseline (`Verified_Rehydration_Agent_README.md:71`). The non-normative Whitepaper §05 frames the same commitment as 4 higher-level guarantees (state verified before use, failures suppressed, freshness confirmed, safe mode on failure) at `Verified_Rehydration_Agent_Whitepaper.html:1128`.

## Q2. What does the buyer-contract explicitly delegate to deployment?

The buyer's engineering team builds 5 deployment artifacts that the pack does not contain and that I do not implement here (`Verified_Rehydration_Agent_README.md:85`): a stable snapshot facility (snapshot lock or deterministic equivalent with binding evidence); an anti-rollback mechanism (a non-regressing counter or equivalent anchor); a local, versioned policy bundle binding `policy_id`, `V_POLICY`, and `V_COMPAT`; a single-writer persistence control plane emitting writer-lock and contention evidence; and a readmission authority workflow (an `approver_set`, recovery workflow, readmission-token evidence, and release procedure). These correspond one-to-one to the §16 Deployment obligations table.

## Q3. How does the conformance test suite partition?

The Conformance Tests hold 29 records anchored 1:1 to the specification: 26 directly runnable on the workflow-transition and evidence projection surface, 1 parameterized against operator-declared inputs (PT-024, the authority-release branch), and 2 declarative bounded-external checks attested at the release-acceptance gate (PT-028 binding `V_COMPAT`, PT-029 binding `V_WRITER_LOCK`) (`Verified_Rehydration_Agent_README.md:99`; `Verified_Rehydration_Agent_Conformance_Tests.md:518`).

## Q4. Where is the boundary between spec-derivable and deployment-substrate PTs?

The workflow-transition records (PT-001..PT-015, PT-022, PT-023, PT-024, PT-027) and the verdict-taxonomy records are spec-derivable from §12 and §6 through the `next_state(current_state, trigger, validator_passed)` projection surface (`Verified_Rehydration_Agent_Conformance_Tests.md:510`). The evidence records (PT-016..PT-021, PT-025, PT-026) assert field-presence and producer tags whose structure is spec-derivable from §14, while the concrete VALUES those fields would carry originate in the deployment substrate. The boundary is drawn explicitly by the Minimal Run Note: PT-028 (`V_COMPAT`, policy-compatibility enforcement in `import_admissibility`) and PT-029 (`V_WRITER_LOCK`, single-writer discipline in the persistence control plane) bind to deployment-substrate enforcement points the pack does not provide, and the note states these two obligations "must be proved by architecture evidence rather than by the local workflow transition surface alone" (`Verified_Rehydration_Agent_Conformance_Tests.md:518`); they are the two legitimate SKIPs recorded with attestation notes.
